Captain Dua Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version: Captain Dua has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not collect personal information about you or your child. It has no ads and no tracking. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Captain Dua is published by Zain Adtani. Questions? Email za.backup1@gmail.com.

What the app remembers on your device

  • The age track a grown-up picked
  • Whether captions are on
  • Where a child stopped reading a book
  • Stars and check marks earned by finishing a story, a book or a quick game in the city
  • A nickname and hero color a child picks, and the sound and music settings
  • Story ideas a child made in the Studio

The app plays original sounds and music. It does not use the microphone and does not record anything. A child's nickname is typed on the device and stays there.

This stays on the device only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, voice, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any information about children. The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics tools. The city shows day or night using your device's clock. That clock reading never leaves your device.

Children

Captain Dua is made for children ages 2 to 10, with a grown-up choosing the settings. A simple grown-up lock protects the Grown-ups area. Because the app does not collect personal information, there is nothing about your child for us to store, share, or sell.

Deleting what is saved

Open the Grown-ups area (Town Hall), pass the lock, and choose Delete everything saved here. You can also delete the app, which removes everything it saved.

Apple and TestFlight

If you install through Apple's TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect its own information under Apple's privacy policy, such as crash reports or feedback you choose to send. We do not control that.

Changes

If we ever add accounts, purchases, or anything that collects information, we will update this page first and ask a grown-up before it is turned on.
