StoryPond Privacy Policy

Effective: October 24, 2026

The short version: StoryPond has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not collect any data about you or your child. It has no ads and no tracking. Everything it remembers, including the stories your child writes, stays on your device.

Who we are

StoryPond is published by Adtani Education Ventures LLC, a Texas limited liability company, 1909 Central Drive, Suite 201, Bedford, TX 76021. Questions? Email zainadtani@gmail.com.

What the app remembers on your device

  • The age track a grown-up picked
  • Stories your child writes in Create (the “My Stories” shelf): the friend, place and lesson they picked, a title and the pages they typed
  • Where a child stopped reading in each book
  • Stars and stickers earned by finishing a book or a quick game
  • A nickname (letters only, up to 12) and a color a child picks for their name tag
  • The sound setting

All of this stays on the device and in the device's own backups, which your family controls. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not use the microphone or the camera and does not record anything.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, voice, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any information about children. The app has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking. The app does not need an internet connection to work. Nothing a child types, including the stories they write, leaves their device. There is no way to upload or share a story from the app.

Links to web pages

Inside the Grown-ups area (behind a grown-up lock) there are two buttons that open this privacy page and our support page in your web browser. Your browser makes a normal web visit to these pages, which are hosted by GitHub Pages. Under some books there may be a “Get the paperback” button for grown-ups. It shows the same grown-up lock first and then opens the book’s Amazon page in your web browser, where Amazon’s own privacy policy applies. Nothing is sold inside the app. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

StoryPond is made for young children, all around the world, with a grown-up choosing the settings. A simple grown-up lock protects the Grown-ups area. It is meant to keep young children out, it is not identity verification. Because the app does not collect personal information, there is nothing about your child for us to store, share, or sell.

Deleting what is saved

Open the Grown-ups area (the lock icon at the top right of the home screen), pass the lock, and choose Delete everything saved here. A single story can also be deleted from the story itself. You can also delete the app, which removes everything it saved.

Apple

If you install through the App Store, Apple may collect its own information under Apple's privacy policy, such as crash reports or feedback you choose to send. We do not control that.

Changes

If we ever add accounts, purchases, sharing, or anything that collects information, we will update this page first and ask a grown-up before it is turned on.
