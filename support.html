Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Captain Dua has no accounts and no sign-in.

How do I delete what the app saved?

Go to Town Hall, pass the grown-up lock, and tap Delete everything saved here. Deleting the app also removes everything.

Why does the app say "demo"?

This is an early version. The videos and books are samples while real episodes are being made.

Is anything sent about my child?

No. See our Privacy Policy.
