Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. StoryPond has no accounts and no sign-in.

How do I delete what the app saved?

Tap the lock icon at the top right of the home screen, pass the grown-up lock, and tap Delete everything saved here. Deleting the app also removes everything.

How do I change the age track?

Open the Grown-ups area (the same lock icon), pass the lock, and pick a different age track.

Where are the stories my child writes?

On this device only, on the My Stories shelf. They are never uploaded or shared. Open a story to delete it, or delete everything from the Grown-ups area.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Everything in the app works without a connection.

Is anything sent about my child?

No. See our Privacy Policy.

What is coming next?

Weekly episodes with Pip, Dottie, Sheldon and Luma, more stories, and the StoryPond Club. They are marked “Coming soon” in the app until they are ready.
